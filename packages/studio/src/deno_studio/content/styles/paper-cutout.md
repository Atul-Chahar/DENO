# Paper cut-out
- Every element is a paper layer with a torn or cut edge, a soft contact shadow and paper texture.
- Animate on twos (12 fps of change inside 24/30 fps) with a seeded 1–2 px wobble per layer.
- Parallax: 4–5 depth layers; the camera moves sideways through them.
- Colours: muted brand tints on off-white paper.
- Avoid: perfect vector edges, glossy gradients, constant wobble on text.
