# Vertical captions
- 9:16. Captions centre-left of the frame, inside the middle 80%, never in the bottom 15%.
- 1–4 words per caption, popping on the spoken word with a 6-frame overshoot; the current word in the accent.
- Background: the founder's footage, product screens, or a calm brand field with slow drift.
- A visual change every 2–3 s (cut, zoom, insert) so the picture is never static.
- Avoid: emoji spam, every word a different colour, captions running ahead of the voice.
