# Risograph print
- Two or three spot inks (from the brand where possible), overprinting where they cross.
- Halftone dots for tone; 1–3 px seeded misregistration per ink layer (static per shot, not jittering).
- Heavy condensed display type; shapes cut like stencils.
- Grain is printed into the still, not animated.
- Avoid: full-colour photos, smooth gradients, glow.
