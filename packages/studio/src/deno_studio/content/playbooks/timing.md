# Timing and attention

- **Hook in the first second.** Open on the result or the pain, not a logo. Most of the best-liked launch films
  open on the finished thing.
- **Length.** Launch and feature films: 15–30 s. Something new every 1–4 s. Explainers: 30–60 s.
- **Reads.** You know what happens because you wrote the code; the viewer doesn't. Each read needs time to be
  found, understood and registered. One read at a time. Fast actions, slow meanings. Lead the eye before an
  important read. Let the reads set the shot length, not a fixed rhythm.
- **Reading time.** About 0.3 s per word on screen, never under 1 s per caption. At most 8 words on screen at
  once for anything the viewer must read.
- **Rhythm.** Alternate dense and calm moments. No dead time, no scene sitting still, no rushed middle.
- **Music.** Cuts on downbeats, hits on beats, big changes on bar lines.
- **Ending.** Hold the last read 0.8–1.2 s. Rhyme the ending with the opening when you can.
