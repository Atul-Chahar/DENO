# Style

- **One named style** from `list_styles`, applied fully. One name beats five adjectives.
- **Palette:** the brand kit's colours. One accent, owned by one thing or one moment. Avoid pure #000 and #FFF.
- **Real product UI.** Use real screenshots unchanged, or rebuild the UI faithfully in HTML. Never invent
  features, numbers or screens.
- **Layers.** Give the frame depth: background, mid, subject, foreground. Every surface gets base, texture, edge.

## Default avoid-list (applies to every style)
Particle bursts · lens flares · neon glow · RGB split · glitch tearing over text · bouncy easing on everything ·
decorative gradients · purple/cyan "AI" palettes · glowing brains · Matrix rain · stock "AI" imagery ·
everything fading in · centred title on a gradient as the opener · a logo intro before the hook ·
text that repeats the picture · animated film grain (it breaks platform re-encodes).
