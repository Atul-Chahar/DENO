# Type and text on screen

- **Show, don't write.** A sign that repeats what the picture already says is the classic failure.
- One display family and one UI or mono family (plus an optional serif for quotes). Use the brand kit's fonts.
  Real kerning, real punctuation (’ “ ” —), no fake bold.
- **Scale.** Big and present at the hook; caption-sized elsewhere. Compose a calm area where text lands.
- **Safe areas.** Keep a 5% margin everywhere. Vertical 9:16: keep text in the middle 80% of the width and out of
  the bottom 15% and top 10% (platform buttons and captions sit there).
- **Phone test.** Text must be readable at 360 px wide (`studio sheet --phone`). Roughly: body text at least 4% of
  frame height on 9:16, 3% on 16:9.
- **Numbers.** Every number on screen must come from the founder's facts (Story Bank, validation evidence,
  their own metrics). Never invent or round up a statistic. `check_copy` flags numbers it can't trace.
