# 30 s narrated explainer
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–4 s | The pain, in the ICP's words (from `get_founder_context` evidence) |
| 4–9 s | What the product is, one sentence |
| 9–24 s | Three steps, ~5 s each, each with a visible result |
| 24–28 s | Proof: a real number or quote |
| 28–30 s | Name + URL |
~75 words of voiceover. Mark `{cue}` words for each visual hit.
