# 15 s showreel
Best for: brand energy, dev tools, a launch day post on X. Weak for explaining.

| Time | Shot | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | Open on the product doing its most impressive thing, mid-motion | "this is fast/smart" |
| 1–8 s | 4–6 morphing states of real UI, one per 2 beats; a command menu or cursor drives them | each state's result |
| 8–12 s | One metric card or before/after (real number only) | the proof |
| 12–15 s | Everything collapses into the logo on the downbeat; URL holds 1 s | the name |

Sound: a 120–124 BPM track, a hit on every state change, sub-bass under the logo.
