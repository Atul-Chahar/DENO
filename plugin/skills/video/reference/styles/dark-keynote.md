# Dark keynote
- Ground: near-black from the brand (never #000), soft vignette. Type in near-white.
- **One giant number** at about 30% of frame height when there's a metric; it counts up on a heavy spring.
- One accent colour, used only on the thing that matters in each shot.
- Ease `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` or a heavy spring; each move takes 30–45 frames at 30 fps.
- Product UI floats as a lit plane with a long soft shadow; the camera pushes in slowly.
- Avoid: glow halos, lens flares, starfields, neon outlines.
