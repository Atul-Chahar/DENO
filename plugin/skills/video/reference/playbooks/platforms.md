# Platform specs

_Ratios, lengths, safe areas and caption habits for Reels, Shorts, TikTok, LinkedIn, X and YouTube._

# Platform specs (check the platform's own help pages before a launch; they change)

| Platform | Ratio | Sweet spot | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 9:16, 1080×1920 | 15–30 s | keep text out of the bottom 20% and the right edge (buttons) |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16, 1080×1920 | 20–45 s | loops well; title matters |
| TikTok | 9:16, 1080×1920 | 15–35 s | captions burned in; bottom 15% covered by UI |
| LinkedIn feed | 1:1 or 4:5, 1080×1080 / 1080×1350; 16:9 also fine | 30–60 s | upload `.srt`; most watch muted |
| X | 16:9 1920×1080 or 1:1 | 15–45 s | first frame is the thumbnail |
| YouTube | 16:9 1920×1080 | any | chapters, `.srt`, custom thumbnail 1280×720 |
| Landing page hero | 16:9, loop | 6–20 s | silent, seamless loop, small file |
| LinkedIn carousel | PDF, 1080×1350 per page | 6–10 pages | one idea per page, big type |
