# Pre-production

## Ask once, then work
Ask the founder these in one message, with your suggested answer for each, and accept "use your defaults":
1. **The one takeaway.** What must a muted viewer understand by the end?
2. **Length and shape.** Seconds, and aspect ratio(s): 9:16 (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), 1:1 or 4:5 (feeds),
   16:9 (X, LinkedIn, YouTube, landing page).
3. **Facts and assets.** Real numbers, real screenshots, the product URL, a logo. Use only these.
4. **Sound.** Their own track, a library track, a synthesized bed, voiceover, or silent with captions.
5. **One named style** (see `list_styles`). One name beats five adjectives.

Pull `get_founder_context` first: the validated pains, the ICP's own words and the Story Bank answer most of this.

## Listen first
If there is audio, run `studio beats` (and `studio captions` for voice or lyrics) and tell the founder what you
hear: tempo, sections, where the big moments are. Picture changes land on beats, big moments on downbeats.

## The treatment (approval gate)
Write `TREATMENT.md` before any code:
- **Logline:** "X wants ___, but ___, so ___" (or, for a product film: problem → turn → proof → ask).
- **Look:** the style, palette with one accent and what owns it, type, texture.
- **Motif:** one recurring thing that changes across the film.
- **Shots:** for each, `start–end · what we see · the EVENT · transition out · camera · sound`.
- **Reads:** for each shot, what the viewer must understand and when. One read at a time.
- **Copy:** every word that appears on screen, already checked with `check_copy`.
- **Facts used:** each number or claim and where it came from. Anything unsure goes to `NOTES.md`, not the film.

Show it and wait for "go". Then build the hardest 2–4 seconds first as a motion sample and show a strip.
